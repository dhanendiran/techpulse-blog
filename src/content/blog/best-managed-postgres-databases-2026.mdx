## The Verdict

* Choose **Neon** if you are building with Next.js, Remix, or serverless workers and need instant branching for preview environments.
* Choose **Supabase** if you want an all-in-one backend with built-in Auth, Storage, and pgvector.
* Choose **AWS RDS** only if you are already deeply locked into the AWS enterprise ecosystem.
