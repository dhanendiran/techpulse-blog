Choosing where to deploy your software project is one of the most critical decisions a technical founder or developer faces. Overpay for serverless compute, and you risk a surprise $4,000 monthly bill. Go with complex bare metal prematurely, and you will spend weekends troubleshooting systemd services instead of writing features.

In this guide, we evaluate the best cloud infrastructure providers based on real-world cost predictability, latency benchmarks, and developer velocity.

## Which Host Should You Choose?

* **For Static Blogs, Documentation & Portfolios**: Choose **Cloudflare Pages** or **Vercel** (both are 100% free for static Astro sites).
* **For Backend APIs, Docker Compose & Microservices**: Deploy on **Hetzner Cloud** to keep monthly infrastructure costs under $10.
* **For Team Production Stacks**: Use **DigitalOcean** or **AWS** for compliance and managed database clusters.
