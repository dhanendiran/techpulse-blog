The tech industry has a persistent addiction to premature scaling. Every week, solo founders spend two weeks configuring Helm charts, ingress controllers, cert-managers, and multi-node Kubernetes clusters—for an app that currently has three active daily users.

In this architectural critique and practical guide, we make the case for why **Docker Compose + Traefik/Caddy on a single $10/month VPS** is all you need to reach your first $10,000 in monthly recurring revenue.

---

## The Hidden Tax of Kubernetes

While Kubernetes is indispensable for 200-engineer engineering organizations orchestrating hundreds of microservices, running it as a solo developer introduces massive operational friction:

1. **Memory Overhead**: The Kubernetes control plane (etcd, kube-apiserver, kube-scheduler, kubelet) easily consumes 2GB–4GB of RAM before your app even boots.
2. **Context Switching**: You spend hours debugging crashloopbackoffs and service CIDR overlaps rather than shipping core user features.
3. **Financial Waste**: Managed Kubernetes (EKS / GKE) carries a base cluster fee of ~$73/month just for the control plane.

## The 99% Solution: Production Docker Compose

With modern Linux tooling, Docker Compose is completely production-ready. Here is a production-grade blueprint with automated SSL termination:

```yaml
version: '3.8'

services:
  caddy:
    image: caddy:2-alpine
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "80:80"
      - "443:443"
    volumes:
      - ./Caddyfile:/etc/caddy/Caddyfile:ro
      - caddy_data:/data
      - caddy_config:/config
    networks:
      - web

  api:
    build: ./backend
    restart: unless-stopped
    environment:
      - DATABASE_URL=postgres://user:password@db:5432/production
      - NODE_ENV=production
    networks:
      - web
      - internal

  db:
    image: postgres:16-alpine
    restart: unless-stopped
    volumes:
      - postgres_data:/var/lib/postgresql/data
    environment:
      POSTGRES_DB: production
      POSTGRES_USER: user
      POSTGRES_PASSWORD: password
    networks:
      - internal

volumes:
  caddy_data:
  caddy_config:
  postgres_data:

networks:
  web:
  internal:
```

### What You Get:
* Automated Let's Encrypt SSL certificates managed by Caddy.
* Isolated internal network for database security.
* Single-command zero-downtime rolling deploys: `docker compose up -d --build`.
* Total monthly cost: **$5 - $10 on Hetzner or DigitalOcean**.

## When SHOULD You Upgrade to Kubernetes?

Only consider migrating from Docker Compose to Kubernetes when:
1. You have dedicated DevOps engineers on payroll.
2. You require automated horizontal pod autoscaling based on dynamic queue depths.
3. Your database and storage layers are already decoupled to managed cloud providers.

Until then, keep your stack lean, your bills near zero, and your development velocity at maximum speed.
