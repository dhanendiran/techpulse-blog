## Summary Verdict

* If your budget allows and you need **local LLM execution and unmatched battery life**, get the **MacBook Pro 16" M4 Max**.
* If you prioritize **native Linux support and lightweight portability**, choose the **ThinkPad X1 Carbon**.
* If you support the **right to repair and want future modular upgrades**, pick the **Framework 16**.
